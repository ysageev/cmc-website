# Site Visit Report — Schedule Dates & KPI Reference Guide (For Consultant Review)

## Purpose of This Document
<div className="purpose-box">
<p><strong>Objective:</strong> To get feedback from consultants about schedule information in Site Visit Reports.</p>
<div className="purpose-grid">
<div>
<div className="purpose-subhead">In particular, we are looking for:</div>
<ol className="purpose-list">
<li><strong>Precise definition of terms</strong> across all schedule sections and lender supplements.</li>
<li><strong>Desired logic for determining derived dates</strong> (overall completion forecasts, Building/TI roll-ups, and Change Order time extensions).</li>
<li><strong>Consistent display rules</strong> between Standard Single Shell projects and Multiple Buildings / TI projects.</li>
<li><strong>Elimination of ambiguities</strong> where report wording or variance math can be interpreted in more than one way.</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div>
<div className="purpose-subhead purpose-subhead-danger">Consequences of not addressing include:</div>
<ol className="purpose-list">
<li><strong>Lower quality schedule information in reports</strong> (conflicting or misleading dates and variance numbers reaching lenders).</li>
<li><strong>Lower accuracy AI review of reports</strong> (false alarms or missed schedule discrepancies during pre-publication AI Check).</li>
</ol>
</div>
</div>
</div>

## 1. Quick Legend: Report Labels, Database Columns & Meanings (Grouped by Location)

### 1. Global (`Global Schedule` Tab — Project-Wide Fixed Dates & Mode Switch)
| Label in Report / Struct UI | Database Column Name | Meaning & Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vendor Start Date** *(UI only)* | `CMCStartDate` | Date CMC monitoring engagement began. |
| **Project Start Date** | `StartDate` | Date physical construction began on site (`Construction Start Date` in UI). |
| **Notice to Proceed** | `NoticeToProceed` | Date the contractor was formally authorized to begin work under the contract. |
| **Vertical Construction Start Date** | `VerticalConstructionStartDate` | Date vertical framing/superstructure began (start of slab pour or framing). |
| **Contractual Completion Date** | `ContractualCompletionDate` | Target completion date stipulated in the executed Construction Agreement. |
| **Contractors Initial Scheduled Completion Date** | `InitialScheduleCompletionDate` | Baseline completion date from the contractor's original construction schedule at closing. |
| **Temporary Certificate of Occupancy Date** | `SubstantialCompletionDate` | Date Temporary Certificate of Occupancy (or Substantial Completion) was achieved. |
| **Certificate of Occupancy Date** | `FinalCompletionDate` | Date final Certificate of Occupancy was issued. |
| **MULTIPLE BUILDINGS/TIs** | `ShellComplete` | Mode switch checkbox: when checked, disables Standard Single Shell schedule entry and activates the **Phasing (BLDG)** and **Phasing (TI)** tabs. |

### 6. Report (Calculated Overall Project Forecast & Weekly Schedule Variances)
| Label Printed in Report | Database Column Name(s) | Meaning & Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Est. Completion / Overall Forecast** | `LastBestDate` / `LastBestDateCSLT` / `MaxDateConsultant` *(Contractor: `LastBestDateCTCR` / `MaxDateContractor`)* | The project's governing completion forecast for the Site Visit: uses the **Schedule Progress** tab in Standard Single Shell Mode, or the **furthest-out (latest) Building/TI date** when `MULTIPLE BUILDINGS/TIs` is checked. |
| **Schedule Status / Contractual Variance** | `WBS` / `WBSContractual` / `ContractualScheduleVariance_Weeks` / `ContractualSchedVariance` | **Weeks Behind Schedule** against the contract: `ContractualCompletionDate` minus `Consultant Forecast` (`< 0` = weeks behind; `> 0` = weeks ahead; `0` = on track). |
| **Schedule Impact (+/- weeks)** *(CPC)* | `WBOS` / `WBSOriginal` | **Weeks Behind Original Schedule**: `InitialScheduleCompletionDate` minus `Consultant Forecast`. |
| **Revised Sched Variance / Revised Variance** | `RevisedSchedVariance` / `RevisedScheduleVariance_Weeks` / `RevisedScheduleVariance` | **Weeks Spread between Contractor and Consultant**: `Contractor Forecast` minus `Consultant Forecast` (shows whether the consultant expects completion later than the contractor's schedule). |

## 4. Ten Consultant Decisions Needed (Circle Option A or Option B)

### Question 1: How Should Change Order Time Extensions (`COSchedAdjustDays`) Affect "Weeks Behind Schedule"?
When a Change Order has approved schedule adjustment days (`COSchedAdjustDays` with `ConsultantApproval = true`), Struct adds those days to `ContractualCompletionDate` and displays **`Revised Contractual Completion Date`**. However, **all schedule variances (`Schedule Status` / `WBS`, the opening blurb sentence, and CPC/JPM supplements) currently ignore `RevisedContractualCompletionDate`** and still measure delay strictly against the original `ContractualCompletionDate`.
- **[ ] Option A (Keep Current):** Keep measuring `Schedule Status` (`WBS`) against the original `ContractualCompletionDate` (`RevisedContractualCompletionDate` is informational only).
- **[ ] Option B (Use Revised Contractual Date when CO Days Exist):** Once approved COs grant time extensions, measure contractual delay against `RevisedContractualCompletionDate` (or show both Original Contractual Variance and Revised Contractual Variance).

---

### Question 2: What Does "Revised Completion Date" / `RevisedSchedVariance` Mean to Consultants?
In `Project Schedule Information`, `Buildings: In Progress`, `TIs in Progress`, and the opening sentence of Section VII:
- The formula for **`RevisedSchedVariance`** (`RevisedScheduleVariance_Weeks`) is actually **`Contractor's Estimated Completion Date` minus `Consultant's Estimated Completion Date`** (how many weeks later our forecast is compared to the contractor's current schedule).
- However, the opening sentence in Section VII calls this: *"The overall completion date is approximately X weeks behind the **revised completion date**."*
- **[ ] Option A:** "Revised completion date" means the **Contractor's latest scheduled completion date** (update the sentence wording to *"the contractor's latest scheduled completion date"* for clarity, keeping the formula as Contractor vs. Consultant).
- **[ ] Option B:** "Revised completion date" was supposed to compare the Consultant's forecast against **`RevisedContractualCompletionDate`** (Original Contractual Date + Approved CO Days), and Contractor vs. Consultant should be labeled **"Variance from Contractor's Schedule"**.

---

### Question 3: Should `Revised Contractual Completion Date` & `Underwritten Completion Date` Print When `MULTIPLE BUILDINGS/TIs` Is Checked?
Currently, when **`MULTIPLE BUILDINGS/TIs`** (`ShellComplete`) is checked, the `Project Schedule Information` table hides **`Additional Contractual Days from COs`**, **`Revised Contractual Completion Date`**, **`Underwritten Completion Date`**, and **`Temporary Certificate of Occupancy Date`**.
- **[ ] Option A (Show Fixed Project Dates in Both Modes):** Always show `Additional Contractual Days from COs`, `Revised Contractual Completion Date`, and `Underwritten Completion Date` in `Project Schedule Information` (when populated), even on multi-building/TI projects, and only hide the single-mode `Consultant's / Contractor's Estimated Completion Date` rows.
- **[ ] Option B (Keep Current):** Keep hiding those rows whenever `MULTIPLE BUILDINGS/TIs` is checked.

---

### Question 4: Calendar Days vs. Working Days (Mon–Fri) for `COSchedAdjustDays`
When a consultant enters `COSchedAdjustDays` in the Change Order popup (e.g., `30` days), Struct currently runs `AddBusinessDays` (skipping Saturdays and Sundays, so `30` days = 6 calendar weeks) and labels the row **`Additional Contractual Days from COs`**.
- **[ ] Option A (Calendar Days):** AIA Change Orders state time extensions in **calendar days** (`DATEADD(day, ...)`).
- **[ ] Option B (Working Days):** Keep `COSchedAdjustDays` as **working/business days (Mon–Fri)**.

---

### Question 5: What Should `C of O Variance` Measure on Completed Buildings & Completed TIs?
In the **`Buildings: Substantially Complete`** table, the last column is labeled **`C of O Variance`**, but it currently calculates `ContractualCompletionDate` minus `BLDGConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate` (ignoring `CofODate` and `CofSCDate`). Meanwhile, in **`Tenant Improvements Complete`**, **`C of O Variance`** calculates `TIContractualCompletionDate` minus `TICofODate` (and shows blank if only `TICofSCDate` is entered).
- **[ ] Option A (Use Actual C of O / Substantial Completion Date):** On both Completed Buildings and Completed TIs, calculate **`C of O Variance`** using `CofODate` (falling back to `CofSCDate` if `CofODate` is blank) against `ContractualCompletionDate`.
- **[ ] Option B (Keep Current):** Keep Completed Buildings comparing against `BLDGConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate` and Completed TIs comparing only against `TICofODate`.

---

### Question 6: What Moves a Tenant Improvement from "In Progress" to "Complete" (`TISubstantiallyComplete` vs. `TICofO`)?
Currently, **Buildings** split cleanly on `BLDGSubstantiallyComplete` (`false` = In Progress, `true` = Substantially Complete). However, **Tenant Improvements** filter `In Progress` by `TICofO = false` and `Complete` by `TISubstantiallyComplete = true`—so a TI with `TISubstantiallyComplete = true` and `TICofO = false` prints in **both** tables at once (and a TI with `TICofO = true` and `TISubstantiallyComplete = false` disappears from both tables).
- **[ ] Option A (Match Buildings — Split on `TISubstantiallyComplete`):** Use `TISubstantiallyComplete = false` for `Tenant Improvements in Progress` and `TISubstantiallyComplete = true` for `Tenant Improvements Complete` so every TI appears in exactly one table.
- **[ ] Option B (Split on `TICofO`):** Use `TICofO = false` for In Progress and `TICofO = true` for Complete.

---

### Question 7: How Should Completed Buildings & Completed TIs Affect the Overall Project Completion Date (`LastBestDate`)?
When a project has multiple buildings or TIs:
1. For **Tenant Improvements**, the SQL currently takes `MAX(TIConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate)` across **all historical Site Visits** instead of each TI's latest Site Visit (so if a TI's forecast moves *earlier* on a later Site Visit, the project stays stuck on the old later date).
2. When some Buildings/TIs are already **Substantially Complete** while others are still **In Progress**, all buildings/TIs are still pooled together using their estimated completion dates.
- **[ ] Option A (Latest SV per Item + Active Items First):** Always take each Building/TI's **latest Site Visit** entry; while unfinished Buildings/TIs remain in progress, drive the project's forecasted completion date from the remaining in-progress items (or use `CofODate`/`CofSCDate` for completed items).
- **[ ] Option B:** Keep pooling all estimated dates across completed and in-progress items.

---

### Question 8: Lender Supplement Fixes (`JPM Executive Summary` & `CPC Summary`)
1. **JPM `Is Project on Schedule?`:** Currently compares Consultant Forecast against **Contractor Forecast** (`LastBestDateCTCR`) instead of `ContractualCompletionDate`, and colors `"YES"` **red** and `"NO"` **green**. Also, both `Est. Substantial Completion` and `Est. Final Completion` print `LastBestDate`.
2. **CPC `Contractors Initial Scheduled Completion Date`:** Currently prints `ContractualCompletionDate` instead of `InitialScheduleCompletionDate`.
- **[ ] Option A (Fix Both):**
  - On **JPM**, compare `Is Project on Schedule?` against `ContractualCompletionDate` (with `"Yes"` in **green** and `"No"` in **red**), and use `SubstantialCompletionDate` / `FinalCompletionDate` when populated (falling back to `LastBestDate`).
  - On **CPC**, display `InitialScheduleCompletionDate` (falling back to `ContractualCompletionDate` if blank) on the `Contractors Initial Scheduled Completion Date` row.
- **[ ] Option B:** Keep current JPM and CPC behavior.

---

### Question 9: Unit Phasing Delivery Curve (`BLDG_UnitPhasing`) — Actual Unit Delivery Date & Trigger
On multi-building residential projects, the **Unit Phasing** chart plots cumulative `ActualUnits` against `ConsultantUnits` and `ContractorUnits`. Currently, `ActualUnits` are only counted when `CofO` is checked (not `CofSC`), and their delivery date on the chart is taken from `BLDGConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate` instead of `CofODate`.
- **[ ] Option A:** Plot `ActualUnits` at `CofODate` (falling back to `CofSCDate` / `BLDGConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate` if blank) when `CofO` (or `BLDGSubstantiallyComplete`) is achieved.
- **[ ] Option B (Keep Current):** Keep plotting `ActualUnits` at `BLDGConsultantsEstimatedCompletionDate` only when `CofO` is checked.

---

### Question 10: Opening Schedule Paragraph — When Should Sentence #2 Print?
In **Section VII (Schedule)**, Sentence #1 states whether our forecast is ahead of, behind, or on track with the contractual completion date. Sentence #2 (*"The overall completion date is approximately Y weeks [ahead of / behind / on track with] the revised completion date"*) currently prints even when `Y = 0` or when the Contractor's date equals the Contractual Completion Date.
- **[ ] Option A:** Only print Sentence #2 when the Contractor's Estimated Completion Date differs from the Consultant's Estimated Completion Date (or when a Revised Contractual Completion Date exists), avoiding redundant *"on track"* sentences.
- **[ ] Option B (Keep Current):** Always print Sentence #2 whenever a Contractor date exists.
